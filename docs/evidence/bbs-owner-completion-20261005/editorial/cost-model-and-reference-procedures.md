## BBS cost interpretation

`V3Repository.projectFinance` computes each approved time source's internal cost from the independently resolved dated InternalCostRule (actual slice minutes × internal hourly rate with its own overtime method). It computes worker compensation separately using the contractual CompensationRule and its allocator. `directCost = laborCost + expenseCost`; `contribution = revenue - directCost`. It does not automatically replace the configured internal hourly rate with the compensation agreement or force their totals to match. Read `packages/database/src/v3-repository.ts` around 4250–4347 and 4727; `internalCostAmount` around 2435.

The original, unchanged BBS workbook is therefore a configured costing estimate: customer charges USD 4,672, internal labor USD 1,520, direct expenses USD 230, direct company costing basis USD 1,750, contribution USD 2,922 / 62.54%. Contractual compensation is USD 2,100, USD 580 more than the configured labor costing estimate. These illustrative internal rates are NOT evidence that actual total employer labor cost is lower than the compensation owed. If the intended internal loaded cost is inclusive of that compensation, the example's configured estimate is insufficient; investigate its dated rules and additional employer costs before relying on the contribution as profit.

Owner reconciliation: Economic Review → same project and 1–5 October dates → Labor source rows → compare Internal cost and Worker compensation, grouped by worker/date/category/rule; then Commercial Configuration → same project → Internal loaded cost and Worker compensation dated rules. Identify scope/rate-method differences, including the USD 900 once-per-period allocation and chief's later USD 80 outside opening settlement. Decide with the responsible finance/accounting person which inclusive costing assumptions are approved. A supported new dated costing rule changes future eligible scope; issued masters/finalized snapshots remain preserved. Do not revise native training workbook cells.

Conditional sensitivity only: IF USD 2,100 is used instead of USD 1,520 as the labor basis for exactly the same 42.5 hours and IF USD 230 remains the same entire direct expense basis, direct cost becomes USD 2,330 and contribution USD 2,342 / 50.13%. This REPLACES the labor estimate; it does not add compensation on top of USD 1,520. Employer taxes/benefits/overhead could require more cost. It is neither the app's saved result nor statutory profit/cash.

## Concrete alternative contract references

Finance → Commercial Configuration → select project → Finance configuration → Commercial policies. The dropdown exposes Worker compensation, Client labor rate, Internal loaded cost, Project commercial policy. Exact form fields below are from `FinanceConfigurationSection.svelte`; calculation mechanisms are in V3Repository and domain allocators. These are reference exercises; do not claim BBS browser established each transition.

- Overtime: Project commercial policy → Derive overtime after the threshold / Overtime threshold (minutes), dates; then separately Client labor rate → Overtime method / Overtime multiplier, Worker compensation → Worker overtime method / Worker overtime multiplier, Internal loaded cost → Overtime method / Overtime multiplier. Base rate multiplier inputs are human multipliers, e.g. 1.50, not 15000. None, Fixed rate, Fixed addition per hour supported; worker also Percentage of eligible overtime. Example two genuinely overtime-eligible actual hours: customer USD 100/h ×1.50→USD300; worker USD40/h ×1.25→USD100; internal cost USD50/h ×1.20→USD120. Save only approved terms/dates then review Labor source amounts; merely choosing time category does not establish an agreed premium. Do not invent a new overtime threshold to manipulate work categories.
- Percentage: Worker compensation → Rule type Percentage of eligible client labor → Percentage, Percentage basis, Settlement trigger and dates. Bases: Client labor before tax; Client labor after approved discount; Issued eligible labor; Collected eligible labor. Triggers: Approved billable labor; Invoice issue; Client payment. Relevant client labor rate must have percentage eligibility. Example 25% of eligible USD1000 labor is USD250 on an issued basis; collected basis with eligible USD400 collected gives USD100, provided allocation/scope excludes tax/expenses/discounts/noneligible categories. This is a calculation example, not a promise to pay before trigger eligibility. Inspect actual source/basis and finalized settlement before payment.
- Customer daily minimum is independently configured from worker daily guarantee; project field client_daily_minimum_minutes drives derived customer top-up. Example actual2h, minimum8h, eligible hourly customerUSD100→USD800; worker actual hourlyUSD40→USD80 absent worker guarantee. Keep actual2h; don't falsify duration. Review Daily minimum adjustments and source group. The manual's daily/weekly FULL UNIT examples are different mechanisms from minimum hours.
- Caps: inspect PO cap/revenue budget and Billing readiness/remaining cap; check currency and which issued subtotal/period is included. Economic budget warning does not by itself prove legal cap compliance or authorize an extra invoice. All-in fixed customer price similarly does not eliminate contractual worker compensation or actual expense costs.
- Milestone: Billing stream/category Milestone uses approved, unbilled project milestone records and dates; verify approved source, amount, due date, issuer/tax/stream before draft/issue. Empty native BBS milestone tab means no milestone in BBS, not successful milestone training. Use precise companion reference or clearly labelled separate calculation example; do not claim empty sheet tested feature.
- Forecasts: Economics estimated remaining work/cost are planned estimates. Compare estimate-to-complete, estimate-at-completion cost and expected final margin with the approved actual basis. Updating estimate does not create actual time, payroll payment or customer collection.
- Foreign currency: expense detail → Project-currency amount → Verified currency conversion needed → Review expense classification. Current screen explicitly states a conversion cannot be entered there. Keep original currency/receipt; do not overwrite it with manually converted amount or classify as same-currency to bypass. Ask application support via Help for the supported verified conversion/history process with expense ID, original currency/amount and genuine rate evidence. No self-service conversion form should be invented in this manual.
