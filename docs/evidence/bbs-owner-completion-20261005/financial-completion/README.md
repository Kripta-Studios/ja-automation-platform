## Procedures and checkpoints

**Accounting cut correction and successful October checkpoint.** `BBS-ACCOUNTING-FIX-03` changes only Accounting snapshot inclusion and frozen-child date validation. The invoice's issue date determines its period. Required, authority-validated immutable source/manifest links may preserve older business dates as evidence for that included invoice. This does not move old operational work or costs into the new period. An invoice voided by the period's cut is excluded from that pack; the ledger and native PDF remain retained. An earlier historical cut still includes an invoice voided later. All existing parent/entity/tenant/version/hash/amount/membership checks remain enforced; future children, forged dates, omitted children and outside-period ordinary costs remain rejected.

**Collect and correct a collection.** In Billing → Invoices → Manage → Record payment, enter Amount, verify readonly Currency, Received on date and Reference. Record only verified actual money in real operation; these records are explicit simulated fixtures. Open Collections and reversals. For the specific original payment, enter Reversal amount, Effective date, Reason code (Bank return, Duplicate, Entry correction or Other) and Reason, then Reverse payment. Check Gross, Reversals, Net and Outstanding. A reversal corrects the ledger entry; it is not a bank refund operation.

**Correct an issued amount.** Create adjustment takes type Credit/Debit/Correction, positive amount and reason. Credit produces a negative new draft. Review the new draft, Approve, select report language and Issue invoice. The original numbered document remains immutable. The UI has no direct issued-invoice Replace operation. Approved unissued invoices have Recalculate; do not assume the same operation is available on an issued invoice. A voided invoice is retained, and pressing Create for its same stream/period does not promise a new replacement. Obtain a reviewed business correction route rather than inventing an unlock.

**Dispatch.** Mark sent in More actions records manual delivery and sends no email. Use it only when actual delivery occurred. Send by email requires an explicit recipient and Yes consent. Check visible per-recipient state. Queued is not sent; SMTP accepted is not proof of inbox arrival. The Owner UI has no manual Retry button for this panel: configured processing retries transient errors. Automatic retry pending requires configuration/service investigation; terminal or uncertain delivery requires administrator investigation before another send. Same invoice, recipient and PDF request is idempotent.

**Close a weekly period.** Use the exact Monday–Sunday cadence dates; short arbitrary ranges are not weekly periods. Resolve actual readiness reasons first. Close sources is separate from invoice issuance, customer acceptance, worker payment or project closure. It locks eligible leftover approved sources and queues the period reports. A later source is not silently inserted into an issued PDF. Its customer treatment follows the dated commercial agreement; in this all-in fixture it is not automatically an extra hourly bill.

**Review an Accounting Pack.** Accounting → Generate monthly Accounting Pack → Period start/end → Report language → Generate pack. Follow Queued/Ready independently per PDF, XLSX, Invoice CSV, Expense CSV and JSON. Download the actual Ready files. Review before finalizing shows pending records, unclassified expenses, missing receipts, reconciliation issues and changes since generation. Counts here cover the entire authorized Owner portfolio within the chosen dates. Pending means draft/submitted/returned time, expenses or reports; unclassified means approved expenses not commercially classified; missing documents means non-rejected/non-void expenses without a receipt. Exact reconciliation only proves included figures tie out. Resolve genuine omissions or explicitly establish the approved scope, regenerate after changes, then Finalize reviewed version. Finalization preserves the reviewed historical version; later changes require a new pack version. The successfully finalized August fixture disclosed its advisories; it does not teach ignoring them.
