## Active versus revoked grant and Owner route nuance

Owner's Crew page displayed active Worker 2 and Supplier Coordinator grants and the revoked External Technician grant. Worker 2's Crew list contained its delegated people; Supplier's list contained its delegated technicians; Technician's list had no active worker. The preceding production journey created a one-day synthetic Technician grant, verified access, and revoked that exact grant through the UI; see `browser-swarm-20260925-postdeploy-crew.md` and its `grant-stage1.json`, `technician-granted.json`, `revoke.json`. This privacy audit did not change grants.

Owner received **404** on `/crew/time/{id}` for Supplier- and Worker-2-created entries, while canonical `/time/{id}` was **200**. Owner's Crew UI did not generate `/crew/time/{id}` links for those rows; its role is to manage delegations there. The recorder-scoped Crew route therefore does not demonstrate an Owner data denial in the visible workflow. If Owner later receives a visible link to such a 404 route, that would be a distinct UX defect.
