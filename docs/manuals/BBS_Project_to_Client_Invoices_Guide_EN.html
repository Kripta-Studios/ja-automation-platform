1 · Understand the complete workflow

ItemMeaning
IssuerJ&A, the company selling services and issuing the invoice.
ClientThe customer receiving the invoice and paying J&A.
Customer rateWhat J&A charges for a person’s hour, worked day or worked week.
Worker paySeparate compensation for that person.
Internal costThe direct labor cost used in the project contribution calculation.
ExpenseA source purchase with independent payer, reimbursement and client recovery rules.
  1. Sign in as the owner and open Projects. Set up the client, project, assignments and dated commercial terms.
  2. Workers or a delegated crew chief record time and expenses, then submit them.
  3. An authorized reviewer approves the operational records. Finance reviews billability and expense classification.
  4. Configure billing streams and prepare invoice drafts. Review their source records, quantities, totals and PDF.
  5. For a real client invoice, approve, issue, download the final PDF, send it and separately record actual collection.
Invoice cadence is independent of the rate unit. For example, a monthly invoice can charge USD 600 for each worked day.

2 · Create a client and project

  1. In Projects, create the client if it does not exist. Enter its legal name, display name, currency, timezone, billing contact/email, billing address, payment terms and PO reference.
  2. Choose Create project. Select the client and enter the project name, cost center, currency, timezone and start date.
  3. This example uses Time & Materials (tm), USD, America/New_York, a 1 October start and PO BBS-DEMO-20261005.
  4. Review the optional assignment table. Add people and configure their terms during creation, or add them later in Team.
  5. Save once and reopen the project. Check the assigned team and client details.
Reference/planned hours never fabricate actual time entries. Hour-based hybrid included-minute billing requires hourly customer rates; use T&M for full day/week charges.

3 · Project defaults and person overrides

  1. Open Project → Billing → Configure project defaults. Set customer rate, internal cost, compensation and expense defaults for new assignments.
  2. The initial example defaults are USD 60/h customer revenue, USD 35/h internal cost, USD 30/h worker pay and worker-paid expenses reimbursed/recovered at cost.
  3. Open Configure this project’s invoices and continue to 3. Review each person. This step is in project billing setup, not inside an invoice.
  4. Set an effective date and edit each person. Click Save person terms; changing a visible field alone does not save it.
  5. If matching default rates should become explicit independent agreements, select Save these rates as explicit person overrides.
Changing project settings/defaults does not erase saved person overrides. “Use project defaults in this draft” fills a person’s draft; review its effective date and save to apply it. Same-date agreements are immutable: use a later effective date for a new revision.

4 · J&A issuer and invoice details

  1. In 2. Billing details, J&A Automation is selected by default for the matching project currency. Review it when setting up new or existing projects.
  2. Check billing contact, recipient and payment terms. Selecting a project in New billing stream fills available project/client currency, PO, contact, email and terms.
  3. Select Standard, Detailed or Summary layout, and grouping by worker, day, category, detail or summary. These options affect presentation, not approved source money.
  4. Choose applicable labor and expense tax profiles if configured. The example has no tax profile; do not invent tax settings to copy it.
  5. Automatically preparing drafts is optional. It is disabled in the training project. Invoices are never automatically issued or sent by this setup.
A valid issuer in the app has configured identity, address, currency, effective legal revision and project authority. The app’s validation is not a government verification of the company’s tax registration.

9 · Assign the crew and chief

  1. In Team, assign the three people with valid membership dates. The example accounts have the worker role.
  2. Configure the chief’s crew delegation and its effective dates. Assignment and delegation are separate permissions.
  3. As the chief, open Crew, select the project/date and the delegated workers.
  4. Use shared mode for common hours or individual mode for different hours. Choose category, description and whether to submit.
  5. The chief records personal hours in Time. Crew delegation does not grant owner access or all commercial data.

10 · Record and submit time

  1. In Time, a worker selects an assigned project, work date, category, duration and activity description.
  2. Use the category describing the work: regular time, travel, commissioning, overtime or another available category. Commercial rules determine treatment.
  3. Save the draft and submit it. A time draft alone is not invoice-ready.
  4. Review the time calendar or weekly sheet. To fix an existing record, use its permitted edit/correction path instead of making a duplicate.
  5. For 3 October, the chief enters 3 h travel for technician 1 and 6 h travel for technician 2 in individual crew mode.
Recorded, approved, billable and billed are different states/concepts. An hourly commercial daily minimum does not create additional actual hours.

15 · Separate labor and expense billing streams

  1. At project billing setup step 1, choose One invoice with two sections or Two separate invoices.
  2. A combined stream includes approved labor and client-chargeable expenses. Separate streams allow different cadence and tax profiles.
  3. The example uses monthly expenses and weekly labor. Its first partial labor period is manual, 1–4 October; the following weekly stream starts Monday 5 October.
  4. In Billing → New billing stream, select the project, review autofilled fields and choose what cannot be inferred: stream, cadence, dates/anchor, layout, grouping and automation.
  5. Save the dated configuration. Review existing streams before creating another one.
CadenceReview

19 · Issuer coverage and legal revisions

  1. Open Finance → Project issuing authority. Review J&A’s legal revision and the project assignment dates.
  2. A legal revision preserves the company identity and address effective at that time. Later revisions retain earlier history.
  3. Consecutive assignments of the same revision may cover one continuous period: for example 28–30 September followed by 1 October onward.
  4. If coverage has a real missing day, PDF generation stays blocked. The message identifies issuer, project, invoice period and missing dates, with the place to review the setup.
  5. If a period crosses different issuer legal revisions, review or split the period. The app does not silently combine legal identities.
J&A’s default selection does not bypass currency or effective-date checks. Issued invoices retain their historical issuer even when future defaults, addresses or contact settings change.

20 · Edit a draft and download its PDF

  1. Open the invoice preview. Editable invoice presentation fields are separate from approved source records.
  2. Review the available customization fields, such as PO, texts, terms and presentation. Save, reload and check the result.
  3. Rates and source money come from commercial rules and approved records. Use the correct dated agreement or source correction to change the financial basis.
  4. Click Download PDF · Preview. Confirm period, units and total in the downloaded document.
  5. When a rule/source changes while an invoice is a draft, use its available refresh/review actions and compare totals again.
All three example native invoice PDFs have been generated successfully. Public manual images show their tables/download controls and omit J&A’s real bank footer. Authorized native invoice PDFs retain their payment instructions.

21 · Approve, issue and send to the client

  1. When the draft is correct, the authorized role approves it. Approval is separate from issuance, sending and collection.
  2. Before issuing, configure an accountant-approved numbering policy for J&A and review all issuer, tax, date, source and readiness requirements. The example reports the missing approved policy and stays a draft.
  3. Issue a real invoice only after those requirements are satisfied. Issuance creates the historical financial document and final number. Wait until its final PDF is ready.
  4. Review the recipient/contact and use the available invoice sending action, or download the final PDF for your approved delivery process. Labor, expenses and combined invoices use this same lifecycle.
  5. Sending is not payment. Record an actual collection only after receipt; review unpaid, partially paid and paid balances.
  6. Correct an issued document through the available void/credit/adjustment/replacement lifecycle, preserving audit history.
This chapter explains the remaining real-client steps. Issuance, client emails and payments were deliberately not performed with the fictional training data.
