Covers client/project creation, optional assignment table, dated defaults/overrides, independent billing and pay, crew delegation, time, expenses, operational and Finance approval, streams/cadences, J&A issuer coverage, three invoice drafts and PDF download. Explains subsequent approval, issuance, client delivery and collection without performing them with fictional data.

Stage 1: 35 approved and Finance-reviewed hours plus four expenses; USD 2230 labor and USD 182 expense drafts. Stage 2: 7.5 hours on 5 October, billed by hour/day/week; USD 2260 draft. New rates start on 5 October and preserve earlier invoices.

Public figures omit credentials, session data and real bank footers. Native invoice PDFs require authorized portal access. Screenshots use English application UI; saved names/descriptions retain their original source record text.

To regenerate, open the HTML and its relative figures with Chromium/Playwright and print A4 using `printBackground: true` and `preferCSSPageSize: true`. Check pagination, readability, figures and financial totals before replacing the PDF.
