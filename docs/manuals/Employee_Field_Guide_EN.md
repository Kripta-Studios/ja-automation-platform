# Employee field guide

> Release-candidate guide. Use only the portal address and support channel stated in your invitation. This guide uses synthetic examples; never share a password, MFA code, recovery code, receipt, or session.

## 1. Accept the invitation and protect your account

Open the invitation sent for your own account. There is no public sign-up. Create a unique password. MFA is optional: if you choose to enable it, follow `Set up MFA`, choose **Enable MFA**, store the one-time recovery codes somewhere private, enter the authenticator code, and choose **Verify MFA**. A recovery code is single-use. If you lose access, use the recovery-code option on the two-factor sign-in screen or contact the verified invitation support channel. Do not send codes to a colleague.

## 2. Today and the correct project

Start on **Today**. Select only the project where the work happened. If it is missing, ask a manager to check your assignment; do not record hours on another project. On a shared device, finish with **Sign out**.

## 3. Record actual time and travel

Open **Time**, enter the service date, activity and actual duration, and save the draft. The customer minimum billable amount never changes the actual time you report. Record travel separately where the project permits it; travel pay and customer billing may follow different rules. Review the date, duration and factual description before submitting.

Draft means you can still review it. Submitted means it awaits review. If a reviewer returns it, read the reason and submit the requested correction. An approved record is controlled history: use the correction flow with a reason. Do not delete, duplicate, or overwrite approved time.

## 4. Expenses and receipts

Open **Expenses**, choose the project, service date, category, amount, currency and the payer. Select yourself only when you actually paid the expense. Attach one readable JPEG, PNG or PDF receipt; check that the complete receipt is visible before you submit. Keep the original until the upload is confirmed. Do not use a company-paid purchase as an employee advance and do not upload replacement or altered evidence.

## 5. Daily and Technical / PLC reports

Use **Reports** for the report required by the work: **Daily** records a factual field summary; **Technical / PLC** records the system, work, validation, remaining risks and permitted attachments. Do not claim a test passed when it did not, and do not make a program change solely to complete a form. Customer acceptance is a separate, money-free, version-bound workflow; never sign for a customer.

## 6. My Pay, reimbursement and connectivity

**My Pay** is your private view. It shows only your own compensation and reimbursable expenses. Estimated, approved, scheduled and paid are distinct states; a scheduled date is not proof of payment. Ask the administrator if a reimbursement status or date is unclear. Never infer a rate or payment from a customer invoice.

Use offline capture only when the portal says it is enabled. Keep the device secure, reconnect, and confirm that the saved or synced state is shown before signing out. If offline is disabled, wait for a connection and retry through the portal rather than making a duplicate record.
