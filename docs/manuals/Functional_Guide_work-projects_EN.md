## Public website and workspace navigation

The public website presents J&A services and projects. Select **Employee Portal login** to open the secure application. Choose your language in the header. On a phone, open the menu to see the complete navigation labels.

Inside the application, the light sidebar marks your current section. The account menu contains your profile and sign-out action. Search and the page actions appear beside the page title on desktop and stack on smaller screens. Only the sections authorized for your account are available. Status labels retain their meaning: a pending item is not approved or ready to download.

Secondary panels open when you select their heading and chevron. Time, Expenses and Reports keep search, project and status visible; expand the filter panel for additional criteria. Active secondary filters reopen the panel. Collapsing a panel keeps entered values. Team assignment lists and completed approval history open separately, while pending actions and warnings remain visible. In Finance configuration, choose one commercial policy from the task selector. In Projects, use More actions for client and assignment maintenance. Open the document-registration or Accounting Pack panel when you need to create an item.

Open a project, worker or client selector to search inside its dropdown. Type part of a name or identifier; matching ignores case and accents. Use the arrow keys and Enter to select, or Escape to close without changing the value. Active register filters appear in a compact summary even when additional filters are collapsed. **Clear filters** restores the unfiltered view. Reports use one set of search, status and sort controls for the selected tab.

Use **Go to section** in the header, or **Ctrl/⌘ K**, to find a destination available to your profile. Type part of its name, use the arrow keys and Enter, or Escape to return to your previous position. This searches section names; the workspace search still finds records.

In Time, Expenses and Reports, **Today**, **This week**, **This month** and **Last month** set date filters using your browser's local calendar. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. The other filters stay selected. Select the × on one active filter to remove only that criterion; **Clear filters** removes all criteria. In the weekly timesheet, **Previous week**, **This week** and **Next week** change the displayed week and the register dates together, retaining project, category and status filters.

## Shared calendar, UTC, session and navigation rules

Access — Consult: Worker and Project Manager for records in their effective assignments. Create: each person may create their own permitted operational entries. Approve: the authorized project reviewer or Owner, according to the record source. Modify: the record author while it is a draft or through an authorized correction path.

Sign in with your own account. The portal shows only the destinations allowed for your role and effective project assignments. **Help** downloads the guide assigned to the account. **Activity Inbox** opens a permitted source record; reading an alert is not an approval. Optional passkeys and MFA are configured in **Profile** when available. Sign out on a shared device and never share a password, session or recovery code.

Planning shifts and availability windows use **UTC**. Actual time entries use the local clock in the project's timezone; start and end are stored as entered, without conversion to UTC. In **Projects**, use previous/next month or **Today**, select a day to open its agenda, and select an event to open the authorized project. A project end date includes that whole day. A timed shift end is exclusive. A planned shift is expected work; it does not create actual time, compensation, invoice evidence or customer acceptance.

Use the project and worker filters before opening an entry. If a day or project is absent, verify the month, filter and assignment dates. Do not switch to another person or project to bypass a denial.

## Worker: profile, availability and assigned projects

Access — Consult: Worker’s own profile, skills, availability and assigned projects. Create: Worker creates own skills declaration and availability window. Approve: no approval is created by a self-declared skill or availability window; the authorized administrator verifies skills where applicable. Modify: Worker may edit their own permitted draft or availability entry; project access is changed by the Owner or project administrator.

Open **Profile** to keep language and skills current. A skill you declare remains unverified until administrative review. The **Availability calendar** shows the latest 200 windows. Select a day to open **Add availability** for that date, or select an agenda window to open **Edit availability**. Choose Available, Unavailable or Tentative, enter the UTC start and end and add a factual note. Save, then reopen the window to confirm it persisted. If the version is stale, reload and compare before retrying; do not overwrite a newer entry.

Availability helps planning. It does not grant access to a project and does not record actual work. Open **Projects**, select a day and open its agenda event to confirm the project and dates assigned to you. If an assignment is missing or incorrect, ask the Project Manager or Owner for a correction rather than recording work under another project.

## Project Manager: planning, team and operational review

Access — Consult: Project Manager for active projects, workers and records in assigned projects. Create: Project Manager may create permitted plans, availability entries and own operational records. Approve: Project Manager approves or requests changes on submitted operational records in the assigned project; supplier-origin time requires Owner review. Modify: Project Manager can correct permitted planning or draft records; editing a published shift is an Owner action.

In **Projects**, use the clickable project and Team calendars to inspect the project, team and planned shifts. In **Planning**, select the correct project and worker filters, select a date to prepare the start and end fields, and review them before **Publish assignment**. Times are UTC. Check the worker’s effective assignment, availability and overlapping shifts. A conflict or unavailable window requires a different date or a factual availability correction, not a second conflicting shift.

In **Approvals**, inspect the submitted time, receipts and reports with their source evidence. The queue places records needing action first and keeps approved records available for follow-up. Approve or request changes with a factual reason. **Project approvals** covers authorized project milestones; Finance review decides the commercial treatment separately. Internal approval is not customer sign-off, invoice issuance or a bank transfer.

Use **Reports** to review factual Daily and Technical / PLC records for the assigned project. Do not change a record silently. A returned record follows its correction workflow and keeps the original history.

## Record time, reports and expenses

Access — Consult: Worker and Project Manager for their own permitted entries and authorized project evidence. Create: Worker or Project Manager creates their own actual time, reports and expenses; the Worker records only their own work. Approve: the assigned Project Manager or Owner performs operational review; Finance handles financial treatment or reimbursement. Modify: the author edits a draft or uses the correction flow when the record is returned; approved history is not edited in place.

In **Time**, choose **Log time** and the assigned project. The work date defaults to today in your browser's local timezone; change it to the actual work date when necessary. Enter the category, factual description, **Start time** and **End time** using the local clock in the project's timezone, without converting to UTC. The end must be later on the same day. Enter an optional **Break (minutes)**, or leave it blank or zero for no break. The portal calculates net duration as end minus start minus break; the break must be shorter than the interval. For example, 08:15–16:15 with a 60-minute break records 7 hours. Record travel separately when the form permits it. **Save draft** keeps an unfinished entry; **Submit** sends it for review and does not approve or pay it. A planned date or shift is not actual time.

Older entries may contain only a duration. Their clocks remain empty until you choose **Add start and end times** in an editable draft or authorized correction and enter the actual times. Do not infer them from a planned shift or the duration.

New period reports and your private worker statement show recorded start/end intervals, net hours and any recorded break in their web views and PDFs. Entries without clocks retain their duration; previously issued files and finalized history are unchanged. Daily and Technical / PLC narratives remain separate records and do not acquire an assumed time interval.

In **Reports**, use **Daily** for completed work, blockers and next steps. Use **Technical / PLC** for the problem, diagnosis, change, validation and backup evidence, with the author. Save and submit the report. A customer report and **Client Sign-off** are separate: the customer signs its own acceptance after the reviewed period; internal review is not customer acceptance.

In **Expenses**, enter the real project, actual date, amount, currency, vendor or reason and the actual payer. Attach a readable permitted receipt and keep the original until the portal confirms storage. A pending scan or rejected upload is not usable evidence. **Approved** means accepted for the workflow; it does not prove reimbursement.

If a submitted item returns in the `needs_changes` state, read the reason, create the correction draft, make only the factual correction and resubmit. A rejected item requires a new draft. Do not duplicate an approved entry or replace its evidence silently.

## My Pay and privacy

Access — Consult: Worker or Project Manager may consult **My Pay** for their own activity, reimbursement and compensation only. Create: neither role creates another person’s compensation record; Finance creates the financial settlement or payment record in its authorized workflow. Approve: authorized operational and Finance reviewers according to the source; the account holder cannot approve their own pay. Modify: only the authorized financial or correction workflow changes a pending source; issued or approved history remains traceable.

**My Pay** is private to the signed-in account, including for a Project Manager. Estimated, approved, scheduled, partially paid and paid are different states. A finalized settlement or scheduled date is not proof of a bank transfer. The **Paid** state depends on a recorded actual payment event. Worker and supplier-origin compensation stays outside the Project Manager’s general project view; do not request or share another person’s pay details.

## Preserve history, act on feedback and get support

Access — Consult: Worker and Project Manager may consult their permitted record history and feedback. Create: the record author creates a correction draft or a support report with the source reference. Approve: the assigned reviewer or Owner decides the operational record; Finance decides reimbursement and commercial treatment. Modify: only the permitted draft or correction path; approved history and issued financial snapshots are immutable.

Treat a status, error and review reason as actionable evidence. Capture the record identifier, project, actual date, displayed status and the specific correction requested. Reload after a stale-version message. If access disappears, check the assignment dates and selected filters once, then ask the Project Manager or Owner to confirm the effective grant. Do not backdate, submit test approvals, use another person’s session or change a project to clear a warning.

Use **Help** for the current account guide and the verified support route at **admin@j-aautomation.com** for persistent access, upload or payment-state problems. Include the displayed reference and factual error message; do not include unrelated customer documents or credentials. The portal history, not an email thread, is the source of record.

## Permissions matrix

Access — Consult: each role sees only its effective project scope and its own private compensation. Create: each role creates only the records listed below. Approve: approval follows the source and reviewer boundary. Modify: drafts and explicit correction paths preserve the original record.

- **Worker:** consult own profile, availability, assigned projects, own time, expenses, reports, permitted documents and own **My Pay**; create own drafts and submissions; approve nothing; modify own draft or returned correction, never approved history or another person’s data.
- **Project Manager:** consult assigned projects, team, planning, operational submissions and project reports; create plans and own operational records; approve or request changes for assigned-project operational records, except supplier-origin time that requires Owner review; modify permitted planning or correction drafts; no billing, commercial configuration, accounting or global Audit authority.
- **Finance:** consults and decides financial treatment through authorized Finance workflows; it is outside this guide’s role family and does not inherit Project Manager access merely by knowing this procedure.
- **Owner:** administrative and cross-project authority is outside this guide’s role family; Owner review is required where stated for supplier-origin records or escalated corrections.

Seeing a menu, reading a guide or knowing a procedure never expands the account’s permission or project scope.

## Form recovery and activity notices

**Today** separates every assignment overlapping the current UTC day from upcoming work. An overnight assignment can appear on both affected days. Read the full dates and UTC label; use **Show more assignments** for further upcoming assignments. Planned work never creates actual time. Open a project only through your authorized link.

Time, Expense and Daily/Technical entry forms keep their entered values after a validation or connection failure. Correct the highlighted fields; the validation summary clears as those errors are fixed. During a save, wait for the result before editing. If the portal cannot confirm the save, check the register before submitting again. If it confirms a save but cannot refresh the register, close the form and refresh; do not resubmit. Closing, Escape, Back or reloading asks before discarding changed entries. Searching a selector without choosing another value does not change the form. This protection is not a saved draft or recovery after closing the browser.

Open **Notifications** from the section navigator or account menu. **All** and **Unread** filter the latest 50 notices loaded by the inbox. Use **Mark as read** for the selected notice; opening the inbox does not mark everything read. **Notification details** and source links retain their normal access checks. Marking a notice read does not approve its business record.
