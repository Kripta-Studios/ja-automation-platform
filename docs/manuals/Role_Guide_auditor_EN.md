# Read-only auditor guide

## Read-only mandate

Sign in with your own account. Use **Help** for this reference. Your authorized audit, finance and evidence views are read-only. You cannot create or change a project, assignment, availability, approval, invoice, payment, closeout or security setting on behalf of another person. A link in **Activity Inbox** opens only a source you are allowed to read.

## Trace a record

Start with the displayed project, period, identifier and state. Open the source record and compare its dates, actor, version and attached evidence. In **Audit**, filter the append-only event list to inspect who changed what and when. A missing event or denied record must be reported, not recreated. An approved time record, customer signoff, issued invoice and confirmed payment are separate facts; never infer one from another.

## Financial and document evidence

In authorized **Finance Overview**, **Billing**, **Collections / Ledger** and **Accounting** views, reconcile a summary to its source rows and effective dates. In **Cash calendar**, distinguish expected collection or scheduled settlement from actual payment. Financial documents may have their own restricted access and scan state. A generated PDF snapshot is immutable history; a newer corrected document does not erase it.

## Calendars and workforce privacy

Project or planning dates in a read-only view provide context, not authority to publish or edit. A selected calendar day and agenda may lead to an authorized source record. Do not use unavailable edit controls or a different person's session. Availability windows are not actual hours, and planned work is not proof of performed work. If a record falls outside your authorization, request a scoped review from the Owner.

## Reporting a discrepancy

Record the identifier, date, displayed status and specific inconsistency through the verified audit/support route. Do not submit test approvals, payments or corrections to inspect behavior. If a view is denied or a document is quarantined, preserve that state in your finding instead of bypassing it.
