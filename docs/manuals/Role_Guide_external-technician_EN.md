# External technician guide

## Your limited workspace

Use only your own invited account. The Owner links you to a supplier and an authorized installation with effective dates. Your operational workspace may include your own **Time**, **Expenses**, **Reports** and **Profile**. You do not manage the supplier team or approve hours. My Pay, worker statements, compensation, rates, margins, finance, billing and administrative exports are not available to this profile.

## Record your actual work

In **Time**, choose the permitted installation, actual work date, category, duration and factual description. **Save draft** keeps an unfinished entry; **Submit** sends it to J&A review. A planned date is not actual time. For a returned item, read the reason and follow the correction flow; do not duplicate or alter approved history. Supplier-origin operational time needs Owner review.

## Expenses and reports

In **Expenses**, enter the actual payer, amount, currency, date and work reason and attach a readable permitted receipt. Keep the original until the portal confirms storage. In **Reports**, use a Daily factual summary or Technical / PLC evidence for diagnosis, work and validation as applicable. Do not claim a test passed when it did not. Internal review is not customer acceptance, a payment or authorization to disclose a file.

## Profile and availability

Use **Profile** for your own language and optional security settings. If your availability calendar is shown, select a day to add a UTC window or an existing window to edit it; save and verify. Available, Unavailable and Tentative are planning states, not hours worked. The calendar shows up to the latest 200 windows and a stale edit requires reload. Never use another technician's profile or identifier.

## If a project disappears

Access may end when an installation grant is revoked or expires. Reload once and check the displayed assignment; do not enter work against another installation. Ask the Owner or supplier coordinator to confirm the effective grant, providing only the relevant record reference. Do not share passwords, recovery codes or customer documents.
