# Owner administrator guide

## Responsibility and the role library

Use your own account for administration. **Help** offers all seven role guides as training material, but each user's own Help page shows only that person's permitted guide. Review access, project and financial boundaries before creating accounts or changing configuration. Use **Activity Inbox** to open a source record; reading a notice is not approval. Protect private documents, credentials and recovery codes.

## Clients, projects and clickable calendars

Create the client with its billing identity, then use **New Project** to set name, site timezone, manager, billing model, start and planned end. Reopen the saved project to check its number and dates. In **Projects**, expand **Project calendar**, move between months, select a day and open an agenda event to inspect a project. In the project **Team** view, use its calendar to inspect shifts. Project end dates are inclusive; timed shift ends are exclusive.

In **Planning**, apply the project and worker filters, select a day to prefill start/end and focus the assignment form, or select an existing shift to open its editor. Compare active team assignment, availability and overlaps before **Publish assignment**. Use **UTC** for timestamp windows. A conflict or unavailable period must be resolved before publishing. Planning is expected work only; it does not create actual hours, compensation, invoice evidence or customer acceptance.

## Workforce availability and roles

In **Profile**, select the correct active person. The availability calendar shows that person's latest 200 windows. A day opens **Add availability**; an agenda window opens **Edit availability** populated with its state, UTC start/end and note. Save and verify. A stale version is rejected; reload and reconcile rather than overwrite. Self-declared skills are unverified until administrative review. Use **Projects → Team** for dated assignments and role changes; ending access preserves history. In **Suppliers**, appoint a coordinator or external technician and authorize installations by effective dates. A restricted supplier profile never acquires financial or approval permissions.

## Approvals and evidence

Review submitted time, expenses, Daily and Technical / PLC reports in **Approvals** with their source evidence. Approve or request changes with a factual reason. Supplier-origin operational time requires Owner review. A returned record follows its correction path; do not replace approved history silently. Period follow-up and client signoff are distinct from internal approval. A customer, not the Owner, signs for customer acceptance.

## Finance and closeout

Review effective-dated commercial rules, legal entity, tax and invoice numbering before issuance. In **Economic Review**, reconcile summaries to source rows. **Cash calendar** distinguishes expected from confirmed events. Finalized compensation is not a transfer; **Register actual payment** needs real bank evidence. Use reversal, credit and replacement flows for mistakes. Archive or close a project only after invoices, records, documents and handover are reviewed. **Audit** is append-only; use it to examine changes, not to manufacture evidence.

## If a control rejects the action

Check scope, effective dates, version, source readiness and role before retrying. Never switch to another worker or project to bypass a denial. A stale availability edit needs reload; a finance discrepancy needs source reconciliation. Use the verified support route for unresolved production incidents and preserve the displayed record reference.
