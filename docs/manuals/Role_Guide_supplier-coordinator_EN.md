# Supplier coordinator guide

## Authorized installations only

The Owner appoints your supplier profile and authorizes each installation with effective dates. An installation is a project in the portal. Sign in with your own invited account. **Supplier team** lists only authorized installations and technicians. If an installation or person is missing, contact the Owner; never use a substitute project or technician. This operational profile does not grant My Pay, worker statements, compensation, customer rates, margins, finance, billing or approvals.

## Manage technicians

Open **Supplier team**, choose the authorized installation and select **Add technician**. Enter the real name, optional email and assignment dates. This creates a personnel record, not login credentials. Use **Assign existing technician** only for someone already in your supplier team. Check the selected installation and effective dates before saving. The Owner, not the coordinator, configures login access and grants.

## Record and submit team hours

Under **Record team hours**, select one or more technicians. Enter the actual work date, category, minutes or permitted start/end interval, and factual work description. Save selected drafts, inspect each named subject and submit the selected drafts to J&A. The technician is the subject; your account is the recorded actor. A draft is not approved work. If **needs_changes** appears, use **Create correction draft**, edit and resubmit while the original remains in history. A rejected record needs a new draft. Supplier-origin time requires Owner review; you cannot approve it yourself.

## Operational report and own records

Use **Operational report** to filter installation and dates, inspect approval states, export CSV or print/save a PDF from the browser. Rejected, void and superseded entries do not increase effective totals. This report contains no money and proves neither customer acceptance nor payment. Your own **Time**, **Expenses** and **Reports** are restricted to permitted operational records. Your **Profile** contains your language and security settings; any availability shown there is planning information, with UTC times, not actual work.

## When access changes

Revoked or expired installation access blocks future requests, including your own operational records for that installation. A stale screen should be reloaded. If a technician, grant, receipt or report is missing, contact the Owner with the record reference. Do not attempt finance exports, approve your own work or change another supplier's personnel.
