# Worker user guide

## Your workspace and access

Use your invitation and your own account. Open **Help** to download this guide. Your work is limited to assigned projects, your own records and permitted project documents. Use the language control in the portal; sign out on shared devices. Optional passkeys and MFA are in **Profile**. Do not share your password, session or recovery codes.

## Profile, skills and availability calendar

Open **Profile**. Keep your language and skills current; a new self-declared skill is unverified until an administrator verifies it. The **Availability calendar** shows your latest 200 windows. Use previous/next month or **Today**. Select a day to open **Add availability** with that date, or select an existing window in the agenda to open **Edit availability** with its current values. Choose Available, Unavailable or Tentative; enter the start, end and factual note. The form and calendar show times in **UTC**. Save, then reopen the window to confirm it persisted. An outdated version is rejected: reload and compare changes before retrying. Availability is planning information; it neither grants a project nor records actual work.

## Projects and planned work

Open **Projects** and its project calendar to review authorized project dates. Select a day to see the agenda; open an event to inspect the project. A project end date includes that whole day; a shift end is exclusive. A planned assignment does not create actual hours, pay or client acceptance. If an assignment is missing or dated incorrectly, ask your manager to correct it instead of recording work under another project.

## Record time and reports

In **Time**, choose **Log time**, the assigned project, real work date, category, minutes and factual summary. **Save draft** keeps an unfinished record; **Submit** sends it for review. Travel is separate when permitted. Returned work needs the stated correction; do not duplicate approved history. In **Reports**, use **Daily** for work, blockers and next steps, and **Technical / PLC** for diagnosis, changes, validation and remaining risk. Submit the report for internal review; only the customer can complete customer acceptance.

## Expenses, documents and My Pay

In **Expenses**, choose the project, actual date, amount/currency, real payer and reason. Upload a readable permitted receipt, keep the original until confirmed, and submit only the correct record. A pending scan or rejected upload is not usable evidence. **Documents** shows only permitted project files; a link does not override access. **My Pay** is private to your own account. Estimated, approved, scheduled, partially paid and paid are different states. A scheduled date or finalized calculation does not prove a transfer; paid requires recorded actual payment. Report discrepancies through the verified support route.

## When a step fails

If a day, project or item is absent, check the selected month, project and assignment dates. For a stale edit, reload rather than overwrite. For access denied, a failed upload or a disputed payment state, use the displayed error and contact **admin@j-aautomation.com** with the record reference; never use another person's data as a workaround.
